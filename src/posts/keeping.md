There is something beautiful about simplicity. Not everything needs to be complicated to be meaningful.

Sometimes, removing the unnecessary is more valuable than adding something new. A quieter interface, fewer distractions, and more room for the things that matter.

## Less, but better.

The goal is not to have less for the sake of having less. It is to make space for what deserves our attention.
